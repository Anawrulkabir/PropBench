From new measurements to a validated property model.

PropBench is a desktop workbench for thermophysical property research. Import your data, check it against published measurements, fit and validate models with stated uncertainty, and export figures and models ready for a paper.

What you can do

One project file holds your data, models, studies and figures.

Look up properties
Pure fluids and mixtures; tables, plots, thermodynamic diagrams.
Explore experimental data
Published measurements from the NIST ThermoML archive, compared with models.
Check data consistency
Find where datasets overlap and whether they agree within stated uncertainty.
Fit models
ECS, entropy scaling, PC-SAFT and any equation you write, with global fits.
Validate
Tests on unseen states, physics checks, uncertainty bands, a selection rule locked before fitting.
Report and export
Journal-ready figures and tables, reports, and models exported to CoolProp.

Built on proven open-source tools

PropBench does not reinvent solvers or fitting libraries. It connects mature, tested tools into one research workflow, and adds what none of them packages: consistency checks, validation on unseen states and a locked model-selection rule.
